1. Setup

Two circles, D₁ and D₂, touch at a point T, one inside the other. Between them lies a chain of circles, C₀, C₁, C₂ and so on, each tangent to both and to the next, with C₀ centered on the line L through T and the two centers.

2. The fact

Pappus of Alexandria proved, some 1,700 years ago, that the center of Cₙ lies exactly n of its own diameters above L, whatever the sizes of D₁ and D₂.

3. Inversion

The reason is inversion. Inversion in a circle centered at T sends each point P to the point on the ray from T through P whose distance from T is r² divided by the distance from T to P, where r is the radius of that circle. It turns circles through T into straight lines, and it keeps tangent circles tangent.

4. The reason

Since D₁ and D₂ pass through T, they become two parallel lines, and the chain becomes a stack of equal circles between them, each touching the next. The image of Cₙ therefore lies n diameters above L. When the circle of inversion crosses Cₙ at right angles, Cₙ is its own image, so Cₙ itself lies n of its own diameters above L.

5. End
